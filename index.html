How to hold the cube

Pick any top and front face, then keep those labels throughout. Read each face straight on using the edge guide below the grid. You can tip the whole cube to look at a face. Keep its layers in place while you enter colours.

Cube colour scheme

The default is white opposite yellow, green opposite blue, and red opposite orange, with white on top, green in front and red to the right when solved. For a different cube, enter its known solved colours below. These are the reference colours and can differ from the way you’re holding the cube.
